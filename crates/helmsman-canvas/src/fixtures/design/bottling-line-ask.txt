A bottling line for a craft brewery, 330 ml glass bottles, about 40 a minute.
Stations in order: a depalletiser table feeding an infeed conveyor; a twin rinser (bottles inverted, water then air); a rotary filler with 8 valves fed from a buffer tank that is kept between two levels by an inlet valve from the bright beer tank and held at CO2 counter-pressure; a crowner; a fill-level and crown inspection that rejects bad bottles with an air blast into a bin; a labeller; an accumulation table before a manual packing station.
Each conveyor section has a motor with a running feedback contact and a photo eye at its end. Downstream blockage must stop upstream sections in order without crashing bottles, and restart in the reverse order. The filler must never run without bottles, without CO2 pressure or with the tank low. CIP: a cleaning mode that runs caustic, rinse, acid, rinse through tank and filler with timed steps and a temperature that must be reached before a step counts.
Operators: start, stop, reset and a mode selector (off, manual jog per section, automatic, CIP) at one panel, a stack light, and a horn before anything moves. Guard doors on the filler and the crowner go through a safety relay; the program sees one contact per door and the relay's healthy contact.
I want counts per shift of good, rejected and broken bottles, and the line must recover cleanly from a power loss mid-fill.
