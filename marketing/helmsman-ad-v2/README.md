# Helmsman ad, second cut (32 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps)

The faster cut of `../helmsman-ad`: same story and the same real app footage,
cut to a music bed at 120 BPM. A beat is 0.5 s and a bar 2 s; every cut,
slam and landing sits on that grid, and the frame shakes on the hits.

    ./render.sh            # check, then render to marketing/helmsman-ad-v2.mp4
    ./render.sh draft      # a fast pass while editing
    ./render.sh preview    # live preview in a browser

Needs Node 22+ and FFmpeg, like the first cut.

## What is in it

| Seconds   | Beat                                                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0-4.2     | Four complaints from the site's "From the field", one a beat; a yellow slash through all of them thickens into the ground |
| 4.2-8.0   | "Write specs, not code", a word an eighth note, then the site's lede     |
| 8.0-12.3  | 1. Describe: the spec is typed on a card that grows into the app         |
| 12.2-21.6 | 2. Validate: the real app. Whole app for run, a push to the diagram for start, diagram and trend for the result, then the whole app again |
| 21.5-24.0 | 3. Approve                                                               |
| 24.0-28.0 | What is built: six claims from `docs/index.html`, stacked one a beat, the answer to the opener's stack |
| 28.0-32.0 | End card: the mark slams on the riser's peak, then the name and the call |

Copy comes from `docs/index.html`; change it there first. The first
complaint is on frame 0 because that frame is the thumbnail.

## The app footage

`assets/app-latch.mp4` is the same recording as `../helmsman-ad`, and its
README says how to make a new one. Root time here is recording time + 11.7
s: run at 13.0, start at 15.04, stop at 18.44. A new recording means new
times in the cursor's `press(...)` calls and the click effects.

The camera is three views on `#cam`, each the top-left corner of the app's
1920x1080 frame and a scale (`view(s, x, y)` in `index.html`), chosen so
nothing the cursor touches falls under the yellow band.

## Sound

`assets/music/bed.mp3` was generated with Google Lyria RealTime through
HyperFrames' `lyria-recipe.py` (120 BPM, prompt: driving industrial
electronic, four-on-the-floor kick, distorted synth bass, no vocals),
trimmed so its first beat lands on frame 0, faded over the end card and
normalised to -14 LUFS with a -1.5 dBTP ceiling. It was measured, not
listened to: swap in a track of your own as `bed.mp3` (same tempo, or
retime the beats) if it doesn't sound right.

Effects sit on visible actions only, each placed so its measured peak
lands a frame after its picture (`npx hyperframes audio <file> --json`):
the slash and the flood, the cuts, the typing, the three clicks, and one
riser into the impact under the mark.
