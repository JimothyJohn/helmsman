HELMSMAN FACTORY AUTOMATION

Every concept from the Helmsman brand canvas, eight boards. The chosen identity: the stencil encoder mark, a Saira Stencil One wordmark, a Barlow Condensed descriptor, and safety yellow, black, graphite and white. Rejected directions are kept here for the record.

Stencil encoder

8 rim bridges · 4 hub bridges · free-floating teeth · no enclosed counters

Thin rim (9 u) cut by 8 stencil bridges, an encoder notch in every segment, a bridged hub ring and detached teeth. Every piece is an island, so it cuts as a one-layer stencil.

Logo marks · round 1

Six directions · one color, scales to 16 px

01HELM GEARShip's wheel whose handles are gear teeth. Steering meets machinery.
02RUNG HThe H is a ladder rung: two power rails, one normally-open contact.
03HEADING NUTA heading arrow set in a hex nut. Direction held by hardware.
04FLYBALLWatt's governor, the first feedback controller. Heritage mark.
05SCAN DIALThe scan cycle as a rudder indicator. Always looping, always pointing.
06BRIDGE HAn H whose crossbar is a small wheel. Monogram for app icons.
Ink options on the board: #1A1A1A (default), #1F2A36, #E2541B, #0057B8

Gear wheel · round 2

Same helm, cleaner geometry · shown at 140, 32 and 20 px

ASTENCILRim cut with stencil bridges, floating hub. Matches the wordmark.
BENCODERSolid disc with slots, like an optical encoder wheel. Heaviest, most legible small.
COCTAGONMachined flats instead of a round rim, diamond hub. Reads digital without circuitry.
DMONOLINEOne stroke weight, round caps, detached teeth. Light, app-native, weakest at 20 px.
EHUB HThe hub carries a cut-out H. Monogram and wheel in one; the H drops out below 32 px.
FHEADINGStencil gear with one lit tooth marking true north. Can animate as the scan cycle.

Each card's table is the WCAG contrast of that color against the theme's ground. "large / graphics" (3:1 to 4.5:1) is fine for the mark, rules and headings at 24 px and up, not for body text; "decorative only" (under 3:1) is fills and swatches. Cabinet gray sets its descriptor in #9A3412, a darker rust than its #C2410C accent, which only clears 3:1. Theme 01 carried forward into the identity.
